Heavy Sleeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Heavy Sleeper is a local-first alarm planning app. Alarm plans, bedtime checklist state, notes, wake history, and premium state are stored on your device so the app can power wake routines and morning insights.

Information the app uses

  • Alarm schedules, wake-plan settings, and wake-session history
  • Bedtime checklist completion state and night notes entered by you
  • Notification permission status, if you choose to enable local wake alerts
  • Purchase entitlement status provided by Apple StoreKit
  • Optional one-time location access for local weather, if you request weather in the app

How information is used

  • To schedule and manage wake routines on your device
  • To show readiness, consistency, and wake-history insights inside the app
  • To restore premium access and purchase state through Apple StoreKit
  • To request a local weather forecast when you choose to use the morning weather feature

Data sharing

Heavy Sleeper does not send alarm notes, wake routines, bedtime entries, or analytics events to the developer, ad networks, or third-party analytics providers in version 1.0.

If you use the optional weather feature, approximate latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to return a forecast. Heavy Sleeper does not create an account for this feature and does not store weather-location history on an external server.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Heavy Sleeper schedules local notifications on your device for wake drills and backup prompts.

Purchases

Premium purchases use Apple StoreKit. Subscription billing, renewal management, and purchase history are handled by Apple.

User controls

You can disable notifications or location permission in iOS Settings. You can reset local Heavy Sleeper data from inside the app.

Support

For help with Heavy Sleeper, visit the support page.
