Heavy Sleeper Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Heavy Sleeper stores user alarm plans, bedtime checklist state, notes, and premium selection locally on device for this build.

Data collected

  • Alarm schedules and wake-plan settings
  • Bedtime checklist completion state
  • Night note text entered by the user
  • Basic in-app premium state related to StoreKit testing

How data is used

  • To power wake-up planning and nightly routines inside the app
  • To show wake consistency and readiness information
  • To restore local app behavior between launches

Data sharing

This build does not send user alarm notes or routine data to external analytics, ad networks, or email providers.

Notifications

If the user grants notification permission, Heavy Sleeper schedules local notifications on-device for wake drills and backup prompts.

Purchases

Premium purchase flows use StoreKit. Production receipt handling and account-level subscription management are handled by Apple after release.

User controls

Users can reset local app data from the Settings tab in this build.

Contact

Before App Store submission, replace this section with a hosted support contact method or support URL.
