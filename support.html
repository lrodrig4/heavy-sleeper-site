Heavy Sleeper Support Draft

Product summary

Heavy Sleeper helps users build layered alarm plans for mornings where missing one alarm is not acceptable.

Common issues

Wake drill did not fire

  • Confirm notification permission is enabled
  • Confirm the device is not in a state that suppresses alerts
  • Re-arm the drill from the Wake or Settings screens

Premium products do not load in development

  • Confirm the shared scheme uses Config/HeavySleeper.storekit
  • Relaunch the simulator run and reload products from the Premium screen

Alarm plans disappeared

  • Check whether local app data was reset from Settings
  • If this is a development build, confirm the simulator container was not replaced by a reinstall
