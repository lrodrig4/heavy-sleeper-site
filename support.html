Heavy Sleeper Support

Heavy Sleeper helps people build layered alarm plans for mornings where missing one alarm is not acceptable.

Common Issues

Wake alerts did not fire

  • Confirm notifications are enabled for Heavy Sleeper in iOS Settings.
  • Open the app after editing a wake stack so alerts can be scheduled again.
  • Check that the device volume, Focus settings, and notification settings allow audible alerts.

Premium products do not load

  • Confirm the device has an internet connection.
  • Open the Premium tab and try loading products again.
  • If you already purchased Heavy Sleeper+, use Restore Purchases from the Premium or Settings tab.

Alarm plans or notes disappeared

  • Heavy Sleeper stores alarm plans and notes locally on the device.
  • Reinstalling the app, resetting app data, or restoring a device without app data may clear local plans.

Weather does not load

  • Confirm location permission is enabled for Heavy Sleeper.
  • Check the internet connection and try loading the morning weather again.

Helpful details for support

When requesting help, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Heavy Sleeper app version, and a short description of what happened.
